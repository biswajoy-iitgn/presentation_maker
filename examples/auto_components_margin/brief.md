# Brief

**Topic.** Margin recovery for an Indian auto-components manufacturer (forgings and machined parts, five plants, about ₹6,000 crore revenue).

**Requirement.** The CEO needs a board discussion document. EBITDA margin has fallen for three years while revenue grew. The board wants to know why, how we compare with peers, where the improvement potential is, and a plan to get back to 15% within 24 months.

**Data available.** P&L FY22 to FY25. Revenue and margin by product line. Plant-level OEE, conversion cost and output. Peer benchmarks from annual reports of eight listed peers. Plant loss data for the worst plant. Lever estimates from the operations team.

**What I want to show.** A clear diagnosis, a fair comparison with peers, the size of the prize by lever and plant, a prioritised plan with a margin walk and roadmap, and the decisions we need from the board.

**Audience.** Board of directors. About 13 slides.

*All company data in this example is synthetic and internally consistent. It is not a real company.*
