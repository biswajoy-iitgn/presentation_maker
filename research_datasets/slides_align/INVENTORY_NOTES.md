# Slides-Align inventory notes

Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Yqy6/Slides-Align

The Hub card describes a human preference-ranking dataset for generated slides and reports 1,326 rankings across nine products, seven scenario categories, and 187 topics. Its visible dataset viewer reported 756 rows, so the local inventory records the discrepancy rather than treating the card count as verified. The current Hub revision was `2f50ac6674a506acb245275e58c8a452c00e6a14`. The Hub API reported 57,073,806,859 storage bytes (the page rounded this to 58.4 GB), far above the 10 GiB data budget. Only the public README/card was saved; no ranking payload or slide images were downloaded.

The card declares MIT for the dataset and warns that generated slide content may remain subject to the originating AI products' terms. Rights in embedded/generated presentation content remain separate from the dataset code/data declaration.
